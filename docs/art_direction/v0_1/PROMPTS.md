# KIMOZ — propozycje oprawy v0.1

Status: trzy propozycje do wyboru, niezatwierdzony art direction. Makiety statyczne, nie zrzuty działającej gry ani gotowe sprite sheety. Generowane wbudowanym image_gen, bez CLI/API.

Wspólne przykłady: Hipopotam, Małpa, Orzeł i hybryda Hipopotama z Orłem; arena, menu, łączenie. Priorytet: pełne sylwetki, humor, czytelne role i tylko HP nad jednostką. Wszelkie dodatkowe zwierzęta lub ozdobne hasła pojawiające się na ilustracjach nie stanowią zmian zasad ani rosteru.

Po wyborze kierunku: dopracowanie wybranych postaci, przygotowanie części do animacji, arena i komponenty UI, a następnie działająca próbka w Godot. Makieta nie dowodzi jeszcze jakości animacji, czytelności tłumu ani kosztu przygotowania wszystkich hybryd.

## Prompty

### A — Kreskówkowa zadyma

```text
Use case: ui-mockup and stylized-concept.
Create a polished visual direction board for KIMOZ, an original full 2D cartoon animal auto-battler for desktop. This is a proposed target art direction, not a screenshot of an implemented game. High-resolution landscape board, crisp readable professionally designed game interfaces. Four well separated framed areas: a LARGE gameplay screen covering upper left two thirds, a full-body character lineup beneath it, a smaller main menu upper right, a smaller fusion selection screen lower right. Make this look like ONE cohesive game, not unrelated illustrations. Small clean board heading with the direction letter/name specified below.
SAME CONTENT across this proposal: three full-body animals with absolutely distinct silhouettes: a huge barrel-shaped purple HIPPOPOTAMUS tank with tiny feet and a massive jaw, an agile orange MONKEY healer carrying a conspicuously large banana, and a sharp angular EAGLE assassin with dramatic wings. Fourth lineup character is a hilarious HIPPO+EAGLE hybrid: huge heavy hippo belly, proud eagle eyebrows and beak, ludicrous little wings and tiny talons; biologically integrated original creature, not two heads glued together. Expressive comic personalities, visually funny proportions, NOT generic armored humanoids. Keep legs, torso, tails and wings visible. Repeat the SAME character designs consistently in the menu, arena and fusion portraits.
GAMEPLAY: actual plausible top-down/three-quarter 2D arena camera with six modest-sized whole-body units, two teams, enough open ground and separation to understand a frontline tank, a monkey safely behind it sending a green banana-shaped heal towards the tank, and an enemy eagle diving diagonally with a short clean swoosh. Single small HP bar per unit, teal vs coral team markers; NO cooldown bar stacks, NO labels floating over every unit, NO damage number clutter. Terrain subdued under the characters, decorative plants and stones only at boundaries. No hex grid, no chessboard, no MOBA minimap. Compact top scoreboard with five life icons per side, text "RUNDA 3". This must read as a shippable GAME SCREEN, not a cinematic battle painting or giant closeup.
MENU: "KIMOZ" logo, three clearly readable buttons "GRAJ", "ZE ZNAJOMYM", "USTAWIENIA", character vignette in background.
FUSION: headline "POŁĄCZ", two parent portraits (hippo + eagle), arrow leading to larger funny hybrid preview, one obvious confirmation button "POŁĄCZ". No currency, price tags, inventory loot or unrelated new mechanics. Keep words few and correctly spelled in Polish.
Production intent: limited coherent palette, consistent outline weight, low detail on small sprites, attractive at gameplay scale, 2D assets plausible to animate as separated parts with occasional drawn impact frames. No photorealism, no 3D-render aesthetic, no watermarks, no existing game branding.
DIRECTION: A — Kreskówkowa zadyma
STYLE: Rounded hand-inked slapstick cartoon. Thick smooth dark espresso outlines, wonderfully squashy asymmetrical bodies, big readable expressions, solid cel colors and one restrained shadow layer. Warm pale sandy arena, deep teal interface, apricot orange buttons, lavender hippo. Modern polished independent-game quality, playful and mischievous rather than babyish. UI panels with softly irregular hand-drawn corners and clean large lettering. Funny silhouette and physical comedy are the primary appeal.
```
